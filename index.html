About the tool

About

Night Shift Calculator

Calculating night shifts means keeping track of multiple allowances across different shift types, distances, and schedules — and doing that mental arithmetic at the end of a long period is tedious and error-prone.

The Workflow Coaching and Optimisation Co. is a simple tool built by L/Cpl. R. Marshall for the Portland Division and any other JFB personnel to calculate their night shift allowances quickly and accurately. Instead of manually tallying up your SP1, SP2, Meal, and Taxi allowance, you enter your shift numbers and the app does the rest — giving you a clear breakdown and total in seconds.

What it calculates

  • SP1 — your 3PM shift allowance
  • SP2 — your 10PM shift allowance
  • Meal — meal allowance based on total shifts worked
  • Taxi — transport allowance adjusted for distance, shift count, and 10PM cancellations

Who it's for

This app is for JFB members who need to calculate their night shift. Whether you work short or long distance routes, or a mix of both, the calculator handles standard and advanced calculations in one place.

How to get started

Open the app, enter your shift numbers for the period, select your distance, and tap Calculate. Use Advanced mode if you have a mix of short and long distance shifts. Add it to your home screen for quick access between shifts.

Data & Privacy

This app does not transmit any data and performs all calculations locally on your device. To make the tool more convenient, your inputs (shift counts, distance, basic allowance, and the Extra Hours calendar) are stored in your browser's local storage so they persist across sessions. This data never leaves your device. Use the Clear All button on any page to remove it, or clear your browser data to wipe everything.

Accuracy

This calculator is intended as a personal productivity tool to assist with allowance calculations. While every effort has been made to ensure the calculations are accurate, users are responsible for verifying their results against official pay records and entitlements. The app does not constitute official payroll advice.

How to Use

Standard

🕓

Total 3PM Shifts — Enter the total number of 3PM shifts worked this period, including regular, extra, and exchanges.

🕙

Total 10PM Shifts — Enter the total number of 10PM shifts worked, including regular, extra, and exchanges.

📅

Amount of 10PM Cancellations (after your 3PM Shift) — Enter the number of days where your 10PM days leave was cancelled after working a 3PM shift. This deducts 2 from the taxi count per day.

📍

Distance — Select Short ($950/shift) or Long ($2,000/shift) for your taxi allowance rate.

Advanced

⚡

Advanced — Use this if you have a mix of short and long distance shifts in the same period. Enter your short and long distance shifts separately. The calculator combines both into a single set of results.

Extra Hours

📅

Pay period — Each pay period runs from the 16th of one month to the 15th of the next. Use the chevrons above the calendar to navigate between periods.

🕒

Tap a day — Toggle 7AM (8h), 3PM (7h), and 10PM (9h) shifts. Multiple shifts on the same day are allowed. Days outside the active pay period are dimmed but still tappable.

🌙

10PM crosses midnight — A 10PM shift counts 2 hours on the start date and 7 hours on the next day. Holiday status is checked separately for each portion.

🔥

Holidays — Jamaica public holidays are pre-marked. Tap any day and toggle Holiday to add or remove. Holiday hours are paid at ×2 the hourly rate; non-holiday hours above 173.33 are paid at ×1.5.

💰

Basic allowance — Enter your monthly basic. The hourly rate is monthly ÷ 173.33. Results update live as you tap.

Install

📲

Add to Home Screen — On iPhone, tap the Share button in Safari and select "Add to Home Screen". On Android, tap the menu in Chrome and select "Add to Home Screen". The app will run fullscreen just like a native app, and works offline.
