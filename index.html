      About the tool

      About

      Night Shift Calculator

      Calculating night shifts means keeping track of multiple allowances across different shift types, distances, and schedules — and doing that mental arithmetic at the end of a long period is tedious and error-prone.

      The Workflow Coaching and Optimisation Co. is a simple tool built by L/Cpl. R. Marshall for the Portland Division and any other JFB personnel to calculate their night shift allowances quickly and accurately. Instead of manually tallying up your SP1, SP2, Meal, and Taxi allowance, you enter your shift numbers and the app does the rest — giving you a clear breakdown and total in seconds.

      What it calculates

      • SP1 — your 3PM shift allowance
      • SP2 — your 10PM shift allowance
      • Meal — meal allowance based on total 3PM and 10PM shifts
      • Taxi — transport allowance adjusted for distance, shift count, and inferred same-day 3PM + 10PM pairings from the calendar (auto-deduct 2 per pairing)
      • Base Pay — your Monthly Basic Allowance plus the Compulsory Assignment Allowance, both included in the grand total
      • Extra Hours — overtime pay (×1.5) for non-holiday hours above 173.33, plus holiday pay (×2) for any hours worked on a Jamaica public holiday
      • Cross-check — flags when the entered shift counts don't match the calendar entries for the active pay period

      Who it's for

      This app is for JFB members who need to calculate their night shift. Whether you work short or long distance routes, or a mix of both, the calculator handles standard and advanced calculations in one place — and now combines allowance and overtime pay on a single screen.

      How to get started

      Open the app and pick Basic or Advanced. Enter your shift counts (3PM, 10PM, 7AM), select your distance, and add your monthly basic allowance. The calendar is the default source for Meal and Taxi calculations. Tap days on the calendar to log shifts; entered counts are used as a cross-check and discrepancies are highlighted. Results update live; tap Save Snapshot to capture a clean copy for sharing. Add the app to your home screen for quick access between shifts.

      Data & Privacy

      This app does not transmit any data and performs all calculations locally on your device. To make the tool more convenient, your inputs (shift counts, distance, basic allowance, and the Extra Hours calendar) are stored in your browser's local storage so they persist across sessions. This data never leaves your device. Use the Reset button on any page to remove it, or clear your browser data to wipe everything.

      Accuracy

      This calculator is intended as a personal productivity tool to assist with allowance calculations. While every effort has been made to ensure the calculations are accurate, users are responsible for verifying their results against official pay records and entitlements. The app does not constitute official payroll advice.

      Help

      Mode

      ⚙️

      Basic vs Advanced — Use the segmented control at the top. Basic = one set of counts plus a single distance toggle. Advanced = separate Short and Long distance counts that combine into one taxi allowance.

      Counts

      🕓

      3PM, 10PM, 7AM — Enter the totals for each shift type worked this period (regular, extra, exchanges). 7AM shifts are used for the cross-check and the 173.33-hour threshold; they don't add to SP1 / SP2 / Meal / Taxi.

      📅

      Auto taxi deduction — If a 3PM and 10PM are both logged on the same calendar day, the app auto-deducts 2 taxi units for that day. No separate cancellation input is needed.

      📍

      Distance — Short ($950/shift) or Long ($2,000/shift) taxi allowance rate. In Advanced mode, set distance per-shift in the day modal.

      Cross-check

      ⚠️

      Counts vs Calendar — The calendar is the default source for allowance calculations, and your entered counts must still match the shifts on the calendar for the active pay period. Mismatches show an amber banner with the discrepancies; tap an issue to highlight the affected calendar days. The Calculate button still works — the warning is informational.

      Calendar

      📅

      Pay period — Each pay period runs from the 16th of one month to the 15th of the next. Use the chevrons above the calendar to navigate between periods.

      🕒

      Tap a day — Toggle 7AM (8h), 3PM (7h), and 10PM (9h) shifts. Multiple shifts per day allowed. In Advanced mode, an extra Short / Long row appears for each active shift.

      🌙

      10PM crosses midnight — Counts 2h on the start date and 7h on the next day. Holiday status is checked separately for each portion.

      🔥

      Holidays — Jamaica public holidays are pre-marked. Tap any day and toggle Holiday to add or remove. Holiday hours are paid at ×2; non-holiday hours above 173.33 are paid at ×1.5.

      💰

      Base Pay Inputs — Enter your Monthly Basic Allowance and Compulsory Assignment Allowance. Both are added to the grand total. The hourly rate (monthly ÷ 173.33) drives overtime pay.

      Snapshot

      💾

      Save Snapshot — Captures the live values into the Results page so you can copy or share them. Live values keep updating as you edit.

      Install

      📲

      Add to Home Screen — On iPhone, tap the Share button in Safari and select "Add to Home Screen". On Android, tap the menu in Chrome and select "Add to Home Screen". The app runs fullscreen and works offline.
